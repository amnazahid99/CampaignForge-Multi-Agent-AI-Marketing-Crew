# CampaignForge Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview
CampaignForge is an AI-powered marketing campaign generator.

## Tone of Voice
- Professional yet approachable
- Confident but not arrogant
- Human-centric and empathetic
- Clear and concise
- Action-oriented

## Approved Claims
- Multi-agent architecture for reliable campaign generation
- Evidence-backed research from web and brand knowledge base
- Deterministic quality checks for platform constraints
- Human-in-the-loop approval before publishing
- Brand compliance layer prevents unsupported claims

## Target Audience
Small to medium business owners, marketing managers, and startup founders who need to generate marketing campaigns quickly without sacrificing quality.

## Key Messaging Themes
- Speed without sacrificing quality
- Evidence-based marketing
- Brand-safe AI generation
- Multi-platform campaign support

## Prohibited Phrases
- Guaranteed results
- 100% success rate
- Revolutionary (without evidence)
- Best in class
